Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Information We Collect

When you register or place an order, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address. We also store your order history and notification preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process orders, deliver products, send order updates, and improve our services. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Storage). Passwords are hashed by Firebase and are never visible to us.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to save your shopping cart and login session. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at contact@abdullahstore.com.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@abdullahstore.com.
